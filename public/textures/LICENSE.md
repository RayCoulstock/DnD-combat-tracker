# Starter texture illustrations

Stone, wood, grass, mud, sand and shallow water PNGs are original hand-authored illustrations created for Encounter Ledger (2026). They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No external artwork was downloaded. These deliberately simple seamless starter tiles may be replaced with a DM's own licensed PNG/JPEG/WebP uploads. The application serves static raster files and contains no procedural texture generator.
